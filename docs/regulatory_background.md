# Regulatory background: what the EU registries make public

Collected in Blocks 18–19; all quotes checked against the primary documents (accessed 2026-11-09).

## EU Clinical Trials Register (EudraCT, Directive 2001/20/EC)

| Topic | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Protocol information | "phase I trials, certain details of the characterisation of the investigational medicinal products, […] are excluded from publication"; public information covers "clinical trials of phases II, III and IV" | Commission guideline 2008/C 168/02, Section 3, OJ C 168, 3.7.2008, p. 3 |
| Paediatric trials | Paediatric trials (incl. Phase I) and trials in an agreed PIP are made public | Art. 41 Regulation (EC) No 1901/2006; guideline 2009/C 28/01 (OJ C 28, 4.2.2009, p. 1) |
| Results – deadline | "within six months" (paediatric) "and otherwise within one year of the end of the trial"; "If the clinical trial ends prematurely, that date should be considered the end of the trial." | Commission guideline 2012/C 302/03, Section 4.3, OJ C 302, 6.10.2012, p. 7 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52012XC1006(01) |
| Results – past trials | Trials ended earlier: posting within 24 months (or one year) after finalisation of the programming | 2012/C 302/03, Section 4.6.1 |
| Results – flagging | No results 9 months (paediatric) / 15 months (other) after end → flagged | 2012/C 302/03, Section 4.7 |
| Results – publication | "only result-related information on non-paediatric Phase-I clinical trials is not made public" | 2012/C 302/03, Section 5 |
| Start of obligation | Posting of summary results mandatory from 21 July 2014 | EMA news, https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/posting-clinical-trial-summary-results-european-clinical-trials-database-eudract-become-mandatory-sponsors-21-july-2014 |

## CTIS (Regulation (EU) No 536/2014)

| Topic | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Public EU database; exceptions for personal data, CCI, communication between Member States, supervision; nothing public before the decision | Art. 81(4), 81(5) CTR |
| Trial categories | Category 1 = Phase I (healthy volunteers or patients), Phase 0, BE/BA, biosimilarity; integrated Phase I/II = Category 2 | EMA/263067/2023, Section 2 |
| Until 17 June 2024 | Deferral mechanism; publication of key documents "including the protocol, for up to 7-years from the end of the trial" could be deferred | EMA/263067/2023, Section 2 |
| From 18 June 2024 | Deferrals removed. Category 1, adults only: selected structured data at decision; remaining structured data, protocol and summary of results **30 months after end of trial** in the EU/EEA. Category 1 paediatric/PIP: protocol with results. All categories: start/end/early termination/halt notifications "as soon as the notification is submitted by the sponsor" | Revised CTIS Transparency Rules, EMA/263067/2023, Annex I – https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/other/revised-ctis-transparency-rules_en.pdf |
| Historical trials | Submitted before 18 June 2024: only structured data published; documents "not published (this applies to all historical trials, regardless of the previous use of deferrals or publication status)" | ACT EU/EMA quick guide "Revised CTIS transparency rules and historical trials" (updated 30 April 2026) |

## ClinicalTrials.gov (USA)

| Topic | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Registration and results duty | Applies to "applicable clinical trials"; for drugs defined as a controlled clinical investigation "other than a phase I clinical investigation" → Phase 1 trials are not subject to the FDAAA 801 registration and results requirements (voluntary registration; ICMJE requires registration of all clinical trials for journal publication) | 42 U.S.C. 282(j)(1)(A)(iii) (FDAAA 801); 42 CFR Part 11 (Final Rule, 2016) |
